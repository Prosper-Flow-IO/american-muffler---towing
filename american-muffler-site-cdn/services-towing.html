Questions

Towing FAQ

How much does a tow cost in Gallup?

Rates depend on vehicle size, distance, and time of day. Short local tows typically start under $100; heavy-duty and long-distance jobs are quoted individually. We always quote before the truck rolls — no surprise bills.

Will my insurance cover the tow?

Often yes — many policies and roadside memberships (AAA, Geico, Progressive, Allstate) reimburse or cover towing up to a set distance. Save your receipt and we'll provide the documentation they need.

Do you accept credit cards on the roadside?

Yes. Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, Discover, and cash. Mobile payment readers go where the trucks go.

How fast can you get to me on I-40?

Inside Gallup we're usually on scene within 30 minutes. Response to Thoreau, Church Rock, or Window Rock typically runs 30–60 minutes; Grants or Lupton 45–90 depending on traffic. We'll give you a realistic ETA when you call.

Can you store my vehicle after the tow?

Yes. Secure fenced storage is available at our lot on N 9th Street for short-term or long-term. Daily rates and a clean paper trail.
