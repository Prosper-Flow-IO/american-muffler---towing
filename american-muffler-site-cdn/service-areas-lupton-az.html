Lupton response times

Just across the state line on I-40. Response 25–40 min.

That is the window we publish for Lupton, measured from our Gallup dispatch. Response times are typical, not guaranteed — road conditions and dispatch volume vary, and we'll give you a realistic ETA when you call.

If you're on I-40 at Exit 359, tell dispatch the exit number and your direction of travel — on the interstate that pins you down faster than a town name does.

What actually rolls out to you depends on the vehicle, not the town. Tell dispatch what you're in and where you are, and we match the truck before it leaves:

  • Light-duty — cars, small trucks, motorcycles, ATVs, small trailers.
  • Medium-duty — box trucks, large pickups, RVs up to Class C.
  • Heavy-duty — semis, buses, equipment, Class A motorhomes.
  • Specialty — flatbed for low-clearance or AWD, wheel-lift, recovery winch-outs.
  • Accident recovery — full scene cleanup, insurance coordination, storage.

Quote before the truck rolls, a truck number and a driver's name once it does, and a call back if anything changes. Same in Lupton as it is in our own parking lot.

Across the state line on I-40

The Lupton calls we take most.

Interstate breakdowns

Shoulder calls on I-40 are their own kind of job — traffic at speed a few feet away. We work them with lights, cones, and a driver who's done it before.

Semis and buses

Our Peterbilt heavy wrecker has the boom, underlift, and winch capacity for the biggest jobs — including the ones that block a lane until they're cleared.

RVs and travel trailers

Long-haul travellers break down a long way from their own mechanic. We tow the rig and can put it in the shop at the other end of the hook.

Jumps, lockouts, out of fuel

Not every call needs a tow. Plenty are a battery, a key on the wrong side of a locked door, or an empty tank a few miles short of a pump.

Flats and blowouts

We change it roadside where we can, and we stock new and used tires at the shop when the spare won't get you home.

Storms and monsoon

Heavy snow, monsoon flooding, high wind. We roll with caution — and we'll tell you honestly if a call is too unsafe to attempt right away.

We recover both directions on I-40, passenger and heavy-duty alike. If you can give us a mile marker or an exit number along with your direction of travel, give us both.
