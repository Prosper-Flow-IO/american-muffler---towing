Getting here

We're easy to find on N 9th.

From I-40 westbound: Take exit 20 (US 491 / N 9th St). Turn right onto 9th Street and continue north about a mile. We're on the right with a tall sign tower — you can't miss it.

From I-40 eastbound: Take exit 20. Turn left onto 9th Street and continue north. Same sign tower on your right.

From downtown Gallup: Head north on 9th Street past the Santa Fe tracks. We're a few blocks up, on the east side of the road.

Coming from the reservation (via Window Rock or Yah-Ta-Hey): US 491 south into Gallup becomes 9th Street. We're on your left as you come into town from the north.

Parking is free and easy — our lot fits trucks, trailers, and RVs.
