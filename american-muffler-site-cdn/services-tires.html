What we stock

Tires for the kind of roads you actually drive on.

Northwest New Mexico eats tires. Summer heat, winter ice, washboard dirt, and long I-40 hauls — the right tire matters.

  • All-season — daily-driver passenger and SUV, the best balance for Gallup weather.
  • All-terrain (A/T) — trucks and SUVs that see dirt and snow. Great for the reservation and ranch roads.
  • Mud-terrain (M/T) — aggressive tread for off-road and heavy snow. Louder on pavement, worth it for serious trail use.
  • Light truck (LT) — reinforced carcass for towing and payload. If you tow a trailer, you want LT.
  • Commercial & fleet — rib and lug patterns for delivery, oilfield, and service vehicles.
  • Used sets — safe, inspected, at least 50% tread. When new tires aren't today's budget, a used set gets you home.

Tire services

Not just selling rubber.

Mount & balance

Computer balancing on every install — no shake at highway speed. TPMS relearn included where supported.

Rotation & alignment check

Rotate every 5k–7k miles to even out wear. We'll flag alignment problems so you're not burning a new set in 20,000 miles.

Flat repair

Proper plug-and-patch from the inside. If it can be safely fixed, we fix it. If not, we tell you why.

Wheels

New alloy wheels available — cruise in and see our showroom. Steel wheels stocked for working trucks.

Roadside install

Trailer tires

ST-rated trailer tires in common sizes. Cattle, boat, utility, or fifth-wheel — we've got you.

Questions

Tires FAQ

How much is a new set of tires?

Quality four-tire sets for most cars and SUVs land between $480 and $1,100 installed, depending on brand and size. Trucks and LT tires run higher. Used sets start around $200 for four.

Are used tires safe?

The ones we sell are. We don't sell anything under 50% tread, with sidewall cracks, or with irregular wear. Plenty of shops will. We won't.

Do you do alignments?

We don't run an alignment rack on-site. We'll check tire wear patterns and let you know if an alignment is needed; we refer to a trusted partner shop when it is.

Can you match the tires on my current vehicle?

Usually yes. Bring the car in or tell us the size on the sidewall (like 265/70R17). We'll pull matching options from our inventory.
