What we do at the scene

Five common roadside fixes, one phone number.

  • Jump starts — Dead battery? We'll boost you and test the alternator on the spot.
  • Lockouts — Keys in the car? We get you back in without damage.
  • Fuel delivery — Ran out of gas or diesel? We'll bring enough to get you to the pump.
  • Flat-tire change — Spare in the trunk, or sell you a new one right there if yours is shot.
  • Winch-outs — Off the road, in the ditch, stuck in sand or snow? We pull you out.
No spare in the trunk? We carry common sizes on the truck and can sell-and-install roadside. Call with your vehicle year, make, and model and we'll tell you what we've got.

Questions

Roadside FAQ

Do you answer at night?

Yes. The tow line runs 24/7. A real person answers — no phone tree, no callback wait.

I broke down on I-40 — how do I tell you where I am?

Mile marker is the fastest way. If you can't see one, tell us the last exit you passed and the direction you're heading. Sharing your location by text also works — we'll send you a link.

My car died — should I call you or my insurance?

Either works. Many roadside memberships reimburse us directly. Call us first if it's urgent; we'll work out the insurance paperwork after.

Can you help me on the Navajo Nation?

Yes. We run out to Window Rock, Fort Defiance, Yah-Ta-Hey, and the surrounding area regularly. Roads and cell coverage vary — give us a landmark if you can.
