What we fix

The repairs Gallup drivers need most.

  • Brakes & rotors — pad swaps, full brake jobs, rotor turning, caliper replacement.
  • Engine diagnostics — OBD-II code reading, sensor testing, misfire and rough-idle tracing.
  • Transmission service — fluid/filter service, cooler lines, torque converter diagnosis, rebuilds by partner.
  • Suspension & steering — shocks, struts, tie rods, ball joints, control arms.
  • A/C service — leak testing, evac & recharge, compressor replacement (summer season — book early).
  • Oil changes & fluid service — conventional, synthetic-blend, full synthetic, diesel.
  • Check-engine-light work — real diagnostics, not guesswork. We explain the code and the fix.
  • Pre-trip inspections — heading up to Denver or out to Phoenix? We'll run it through before you go.
What we don't do: dealer-only programming on some late-model European vehicles. If that's what your car needs, we'll tell you up front and point you to the right shop.

Questions

Auto Repair FAQ

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins welcome for diagnostics, oil changes, and tire work when bays are open. Bigger jobs like brakes, engine work, and A/C work — please call so we can block time.

How long will my repair take?

Most brake jobs, oil services, and simple diagnostics are same-day. Bigger work (transmissions, engines) depends on parts availability. We'll give you an honest estimate up front and keep you updated.

Do you warranty your work?

Yes. Parts carry their manufacturer's warranty and we stand behind our labor. If something we did fails, bring it back — we'll make it right.

Can I bring my own parts?

Usually yes, but we don't warranty owner-supplied parts. Ask when you book — some jobs (like timing work) we'd rather source the parts ourselves.
