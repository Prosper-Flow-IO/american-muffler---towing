Window Rock FAQ

Questions from Window Rock customers.

How long does it take you to reach Window Rock?

Typically 30–45 minutes via Route 264, measured from your call to the truck on scene. Weather and dispatch volume move that number, so we'll give you a realistic ETA when you call rather than a number we can't hold to.

Can you help me on the Navajo Nation?

Yes. We run out to Window Rock, Fort Defiance, Yah-Ta-Hey, and the surrounding area regularly. Roads and cell coverage vary — give us a landmark if you can.

Can you respond during a storm?

Yes — and we often do. Heavy snow, monsoon flooding, high wind: we roll with caution. We'll tell you honestly if a call is too unsafe to attempt immediately.

How do I get to your shop from Window Rock?

Coming from the reservation, US 491 south into Gallup becomes 9th Street — we're on your left as you come into town from the north, at 827 N 9th Street. Office hours are Mon–Sat 9am–5pm; the tow line answers 24/7.

What payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, Discover, cash. Net-30 accounts available for approved fleet customers.
