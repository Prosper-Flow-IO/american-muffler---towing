Gallup response times

We're usually there in under 30 minutes.

Because we dispatch from N 9th Street — not from Albuquerque, not from Farmington — we're already in Gallup when your call comes in.

  • Downtown Gallup — typically 15–25 minutes from call to truck on scene.
  • North Gallup / University area — 10–20 minutes.
  • South of I-40 / Nizhoni Boulevard — 20–30 minutes.
  • East side / toward Church Rock — 15–25 minutes.
  • West side / toward Manuelito — 20–30 minutes.

At rush hour, after a snowstorm, or during Red Rock Balloon Rally week, add some padding. We'll give you a realistic ETA when you call.

Common Gallup breakdowns

What we see most around here.

I-40 truck stops

Love's, Pilot, Speedway — semis and RVs regularly need jump-starts, tire changes, and coolant services. We're minutes away from all three.

Red Rock Park

Tourists and locals getting stuck on park roads, especially after monsoon. We know the dirt roads out there.

9th Street & Aztec

Busy intersections with regular fender-benders. We tow and coordinate with Gallup PD when needed.

Historic Route 66

Old cars, old roads. We work on plenty of classics whose owners know to come here for proper exhaust work.

Rio Puerco

Flash flood season turns the arroyo crossings treacherous. We've done our share of winch-outs here.

Gallup Plaza & uptown

Lockouts in the parking lot are the most common call. We keep the response tight in this zone.

Gallup FAQ

Questions from Gallup customers.

Are you AAA-affiliated?

We work with many roadside memberships and can often coordinate billing directly. Ask when you call.

Can you respond during a storm?

Yes — and we often do. Heavy snow, monsoon flooding, high wind: we roll with caution. We'll tell you honestly if a call is too unsafe to attempt immediately.

Do you work with Gallup PD on accident scenes?

Yes. We're regularly called to accident scenes to clear vehicles once the scene is released.

What payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, Discover, cash. Net-30 accounts available for approved fleet customers.
