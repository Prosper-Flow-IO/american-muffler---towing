How it works

Three steps, one afternoon.

  1. Call or text with the year, make, model, and condition. A photo helps. We'll quote a fair number over the phone.
  2. We come to you with a flatbed. Free pickup anywhere in Gallup and most of the surrounding area.

No title? In many cases we can still work it out with a valid ID and registration or a bill of sale. Call and we'll walk through your situation.

Questions

Junk Car Removal FAQ

How much will you pay for my car?

Quotes range from $100 for stripped wrecks to $2,000+ for running trucks and SUVs. The big factors: weight, condition, and whether valuable parts (catalytic converter, wheels, major components) are still present. Call with details and we'll be honest about the number.

Do I have to be there for pickup?

Yes, someone with signing authority needs to be there to release the title. We can coordinate a time that works — evenings and weekends by arrangement.

Do I need to remove my plates?

Yes — pull your plates and return them to NM MVD, or if you're keeping the plate for another vehicle, hang on to it. We'll remind you at pickup.

How fast can you come?

Same-day in Gallup in many cases. Next-day for anywhere in our service area.
