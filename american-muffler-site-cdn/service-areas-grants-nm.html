Service Area · Grants, NM

Heavy truck or RV down at Grants? We run I-40 east.

We're not a franchise. We're not a chain. We're a family-run shop dispatching from 827 N 9th Street in Gallup, with a tow line that actually answers and a repair shop that actually calls you back. Grants response times

Further east on I-40. Response typically 45–75 min.

That is the window we publish for Grants, measured from our Gallup dispatch. Response times are typical, not guaranteed — road conditions and dispatch volume vary, and we'll give you a realistic ETA when you call.

On the interstate, an exit number and your direction of travel pin you down faster than a town name does. Give dispatch both if you have them.

What actually rolls out to you depends on the vehicle, not the town. Tell dispatch what you're in and where you are, and we match the truck before it leaves the yard:

  • Light-duty — cars, small trucks, motorcycles, ATVs, small trailers.
  • Medium-duty — box trucks, large pickups, RVs up to Class C.
  • Heavy-duty — semis, buses, equipment, Class A motorhomes.
  • Specialty — flatbed for low-clearance or AWD, wheel-lift, recovery winch-outs.
  • Accident recovery — full scene cleanup, insurance coordination, storage.

Quote before the truck rolls, a truck number and a driver's name once it does, and a call back if anything changes. Same out at Grants as it is in our own parking lot.

Heavy truck & RV country

The Grants calls we take most.

Semis and heavy trucks

The work we get called out to Grants for most. Our Peterbilt heavy wrecker has the boom, underlift, and winch capacity for the biggest jobs on I-40 — including the ones that block a lane until they're cleared.

RVs and motorhomes

Also common on this stretch. Class C on the medium-duty, Class A on the heavy. Long-haul travellers break down a long way from their own mechanic — we tow the rig and can put it in the shop at the other end of the hook.

Interstate breakdowns

Shoulder calls on I-40 are their own kind of job — traffic at speed a few feet away. We work them with lights, cones, and a driver who's done it before.

Blowouts on the interstate

Highway-speed tire failures, and the drive-tire blowouts that come with loaded trucks. We change on the roadside where it's safe to, and we stock new and used tires at the shop when it isn't.

Storms across the corridor

Heavy snow, monsoon flooding, high wind. We roll with caution — and we'll tell you honestly if a call is too unsafe to attempt right away.

Jumps, lockouts, out of fuel

Not every call needs a tow. Plenty are a battery, a key on the wrong side of a locked door, or an empty tank short of the next exit.

We recover both directions on I-40, passenger and heavy-duty alike. Because Grants is at the far end of our run, tell dispatch the size of the vehicle up front — that's what decides which truck leaves the yard.

Grants FAQ

How fast can you reach Grants?

Do you tow heavy trucks and RVs to Grants?
